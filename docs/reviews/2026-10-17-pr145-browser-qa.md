## Follow-up: original prompts and the nested creation boundary

The exact original create/update prompts were recovered from the source Google Doc and retained in `backend/tests/fixtures/oct14_personal_manager_prompts.json`. The existing real staging/executor/readback regression now retains both prompts in its conversation history.

Replaying the original 3-Track/15-entry scenario exposed a further defect: canonical creation captured its ChangeEvent in the staged command outbox, then emitted the same event again after its local nested scope closed while the worker's outer transaction remained open. A connected subscriber from another workspace was correctly denied access, but that nested admission failure poisoned the writer's transaction. The next Track create failed and the full graph transaction rolled back. The separate immediate audit delivery had already occurred; it was not valid proof of a committed App.

`creation_write_scope` now sends only uncaptured, nontransactional events through its immediate fallback. Captured events remain in the durable outbox and are delivered after commit. Subscriber admission is unchanged. A new actual PostgreSQL regression reproduces the failing build under a held outer transaction with a foreign subscriber, then passes with the fix. It includes 15-entry graph readback and the existing six updates without duplicate entities or broken business links.
