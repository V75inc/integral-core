# The Integral concept

Integral is an AI-native knowledge and operational platform: a common substrate in which people and AI read, write, structure, and coordinate domain knowledge under a shared access model.

Its purpose is to remove a basic obstacle to AI-first work. An agent often has no canonical place to find operational truth or leave an accountable result. Gathering fragments does not by itself establish the structure, authority, or continuity needed to operate on them.

Integral joins three capabilities:

1. **Connected knowledge:** typed records and explicit relationships preserve context.
2. **Conformable software:** Operational Models and App packages shape records, views, queries, operations, and skills around a purpose.
3. **Governed intelligence:** resident and external agents act through scoped capabilities, policy, review paths, and execution receipts.

## The substrate

Core owns generic knowledge primitives, workspace scope, resource permissions, schema validation, graph integrity, extension contracts, and execution controls. Domain Apps give work its specific meaning. Pricing, checkout, hosted offerings, and organization-specific commercial rules stay outside Core.

## Generative software

Generative software can be constructed and revised with AI assistance from an expressed purpose. In Integral, its shape has explicit artifacts: schemas, manifests, views, queries, operations, and skills. These can be inspected and validated.

A conversation begins the process. Authoring, permission, staging, installation, and migration controls make the resulting application governable.

## Commitments

- Bring people and AI close to the same operational facts.
- Preserve explicit relationships and attribution.
- Support adaptable structure while validating effects on existing data.
- Enforce authority in Core rather than interface visibility or agent prose.
- Keep domain applications outside the foundation.
- Report proposals, execution results, and uncertainty honestly.

Integral uses one resident mind per active harness binding, faceted by principal and scope. External agents enter through MCP. The default is Integral AI through Pydantic AI; jvagent is a compatibility binding.
