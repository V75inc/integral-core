# Meet Integral

Imagine asking your software to help you organize a new service.

You explain how requests arrive, what needs review, who makes decisions, and what a completed job should contain. Together, you give the work a shape: a place for requests, a place for decisions, a place for delivery, and relationships that keep the whole story connected.

You can open the records yourself. You can change a view. You can ask the AI what is waiting, inspect the facts behind its answer, and review its next proposed change.

This is the experience Integral is built to make possible: **software shaped around work, with people and AI participating in the same operational world.**

## A place for the whole story

Organizations have plenty of information. The difficulty is keeping it connected and usable. Documents describe plans, spreadsheets track progress, messages carry decisions, and applications hold fragments of the truth.

Integral offers a common foundation. Records have types, relationships, provenance, and an audience. An application becomes a useful arrangement of shared building blocks. The AI works through that foundation, so assistance can refer to the actual work rather than only the text of a conversation.

Information can become the structure of an application. The structure can evolve as people learn. AI can help with that evolution while the people responsible retain control over consequential changes.

## Familiar pieces, a different foundation

You do not need to understand graph databases to use Integral.

A **workspace** establishes where you are working and with whom. An **App** brings a purpose into focus. A **Track** holds a collection of records. An **Entry** is an individual record: a request, note, issue, decision, or another item your App defines.

An **Operational Model** gives those records structure. It describes their types, fields, relationships, and views. One body of records can be explored as a feed, table, board, or another appropriate view without copying it into a different application.

An App package can carry queries, operations, skills, tools, integration behavior, extension views, and a Home that presents useful information. These are declared capabilities with a common access perimeter.

## An AI that shares the context

The resident AI is available through conversation. It can find information, explain a structure, prepare records, and propose changes supported by current tools and permissions.

For example:

> “Help me understand the requests still waiting for review. Show me the records, then draft a summary for the team.”

Or:

> “Design an App for this process. Let us review the fields and views before creating it.”

These examples express intent. A request may require more information, a capability your installation does not provide, or human review. A proposal becomes real when its authorized execution path applies it and reports the result.

A useful assistant should make clear what it found, what it proposes, and what happened.

## Control belongs in the foundation

Permissions belong to resources and are checked by the backend. Workspace selection sets the operating scope. A skill explains how to perform a task; it does not grant permission. An approval enters a particular execution path; later suggestions still need their own authority.

People participate at different levels. Some read, some comment, others edit records or curate structure. Sensitive information needs an appropriate resource boundary rather than cosmetic hiding inside a shared record.

The same principles apply to a person using a view, the resident using a tool, and an external agent connecting through MCP.

## Start small, grow deliberately

Choose a workspace, create a Track, add a few records, and open a second view of the same information. Invite a collaborator with an intentional role. Then ask the resident to summarize information it can access and inspect the source records.

This makes the central idea tangible: structure, presentation, collaboration, and AI assistance can develop together around one body of knowledge.

Integral Core is pre-1.0. The architecture supports a wider ambition than a single release can prove. These guides explain working surfaces and unfinished qualification openly. The direction is coherent: make operational knowledge a foundation that software and intelligence can share.
